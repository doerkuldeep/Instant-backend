## 1. Professional Integrity

All partners, technicians, and machine operators affiliated with **{{COMPANY_NAME}}** must adhere to the highest standards of professional conduct, safety, and business ethics.

---

## 2. Workplace Health & Safety

- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** All operators must wear certified helmets, high-visibility reflective vests, safety boots, and ear protection when on job sites.
- **Sobriety & Substance Policy:** Absolute zero-tolerance for alcohol, narcotics, or impairment on duty.
- **Pre-Shift Inspection:** Operators must conduct daily checks of hydraulic hoses, brakes, steering, and emergency shut-offs before machine operation.

---

## 3. Fair Dealing & Non-Discrimination

Partners must treat all clients, platform representatives, and public members with dignity and respect. Any discrimination based on race, religion, gender, disability, or regional origin will result in permanent de-listing.

---

## 4. Anti-Bribery & Corruption

Partners must never offer, solicit, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or inappropriate gifts from site supervisors or procurement personnel to secure orders.
