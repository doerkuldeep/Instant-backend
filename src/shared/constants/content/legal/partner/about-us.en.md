## 1. Our Mission

**{{COMPANY_NAME}}** is India's leading industrial machinery and construction equipment rental network. We bridge the gap between heavy equipment fleet owners and verified construction, mining, agricultural, and industrial infrastructure contractors.

## 2. How the Partnership Model Works

### Step 1: Onboarding & Fleet Registration
Register with your mobile phone, verify KYC credentials and police verification certificates, and list your machinery specifying rates, fuel policy, and operator availability.

### Step 2: Smart Demand Matching
Our proprietary matching algorithm pairs your idle equipment with nearby verified infrastructure projects, minimizing mobilization transit distances.

### Step 3: Order Execution & Digital Check-In
Accept bookings on the mobile app. Operators track daily shift hours via our digital logbook and GPS telemetry.

### Step 4: Automated Settlement
Every Wednesday, rental earnings are automatically transferred directly to your bank account after deducting our standard platform commission.
