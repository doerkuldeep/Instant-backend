## 1. Principles of Financial Settlements & Refunds

At **{{COMPANY_NAME}}**, transparency in payments between clients, partners, and our platform is paramount. This Refund Policy governs circumstances under which payments, security deposits, or rental fees may be adjusted, deducted, or refunded.

---

## 2. Security Deposits & Deductions

- Security deposits collected from leasing clients are held in an escrow account until completion of the rental cycle.
- Upon equipment return, partners have twenty-four (24) hours to submit a damage assessment or incident report.
- If verified damage or excessive unauthorized hours are documented, the agreed cost is transferred to the Partner from the security deposit.
- Remaining security deposits are unconditionally returned to the lessee within two (2) business days.

---

## 3. Partner Refund Liabilities

Partners may be held financially liable for refunds to customers under the following conditions:
- **Equipment Failure:** If the delivered machine malfunctions during the first four (4) hours of operation due to pre-existing mechanical faults not disclosed beforehand.
- **Operator Absence:** If an operator-inclusive contract was agreed upon, but the operator fails to report to the job site.
- **Specification Mismatch:** If the delivered machinery fails to match the documented tonnage, reach, or operational specifications.

---

## 4. Refund Dispute Resolution

Partners dispute deductions or customer refund claims by contacting Partner Support within forty-eight (48) hours of notification with diagnostic reports and photo evidence.
