## 1. Commission Structure

{{COMPANY_NAME}} operates on a transparent, performance-aligned commission structure:
- **Standard Partner Tier:** 10.0% platform fee on gross machine rental earnings.
- **Preferred Partner Tier (Volume > 500 machine hours/month):** 8.0% platform fee.
- **Enterprise Partner Tier (Fleet > 15 machines):** 6.5% platform fee with dedicated fleet management support.

---

## 2. Settlement Cycles & Payout Schedule

- **Billing Cycle:** Every Monday 00:00:00 to Sunday 23:59:59 IST.
- **Audit & Reconciliation:** Every Monday and Tuesday.
- **Payout Disbursement:** Every Wednesday by 18:00 IST directly to the Partner's verified bank account via automated IMPS/NEFT.

---

## 3. Bank Account Requirements

To receive disbursements without delay:
1. Bank Account Holder Name must match the Partner Legal Name or registered Trade Name.
2. Verified IFSC code and 9-18 digit account number.
3. Successfully passed Penny Drop verification test.

---

## 4. Statutory Tax Deductions (TDS)

As mandated by Indian Income Tax law:
- Section 194C (Contracts/Machinery hiring): Applicable TDS (1% for individuals/HUF, 2% for corporate entities) is deducted and remitted directly to the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT).
- Quarterly Form 16A TDS certificates are generated and downloadable via the Partner Web Portal.
