## 1. Right to Erasure

Partners have the right to request deletion of their personal identity records and deregistration of their partner profile upon cessation of business activities on the platform.

---

## 2. Deletion Process & Timelines

1. **Submission:** Partner sends a verified deletion request to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with registered phone number.
2. **Account Audit:** All outstanding bookings must be completed, and financial ledgers reconciled.
3. **Execution:** Within thirty (30) days of confirmation, personal identifiers (photos, address proofs, phone OTP logs) are permanently purged from active databases.

---

## 3. Statutory Retention Exceptions

In compliance with Indian taxation, financial reporting, and police verification guidelines:
- Invoicing and payout records are retained for seven (7) financial years under GST and Income Tax laws.
- Incident logs regarding severe site accidents or police reports are archived securely as mandated by criminal jurisprudence.
