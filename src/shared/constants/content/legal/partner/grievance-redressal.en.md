## 1. Objective & Scope

This policy outlines our structured escalation mechanism to address and resolve partner complaints, payment disputes, onboarding issues, and operational disagreements in an expedited and impartial manner.

---

## 2. Escalation Matrix

### Level 1: Partner Support Desk
- **Channel:** Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or Phone {{SUPPORT_PHONE}}
- **Resolution SLA:** Within twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) business hours.
- **Coverage:** Daily dispatch inquiries, trip logging discrepancies, and app technical bugs.

### Level 2: Operations Head
- **Channel:** Email escalation tagged to ops-escalations@equipshare.com
- **Resolution SLA:** Within three (3) business days.
- **Coverage:** Payout disputes, damaged machinery deductions, or client rating appeals.

### Level 3: Grievance Officer
- **Officer Name:** {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER}}
- **Designation:** Head of Regulatory Compliance & Partner Grievances
- **Email:** {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}
- **Physical Address:** {{COMPANY_ADDRESS}}
- **Resolution SLA:** Acknowledgment within 24 hours; formal resolution within fifteen (15) days.

---

## 3. Dispute Mediation

If a grievance remains unresolved after Level 3 review, either party may request binding conciliation under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat of arbitration in Bangalore, India.
