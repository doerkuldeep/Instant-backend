## Category: Account

### Q: How do I create and activate my Partner account?
**A:** You can sign up using your mobile number and verifying it via a 6-digit OTP. After verification, complete your KYC by uploading your government ID (Aadhaar/PAN), business registration documents, and police verification certificate. Once our operations team reviews and approves your submission, your account becomes active.

### Q: Why is Police Verification (PVC) required?
**A:** Police verification ensures community safety and site compliance, particularly when deploying machinery to high-security construction, government, or infrastructure development sites.

### Q: Can I register as an individual if I do not own a registered company?
**A:** Yes. Individual machine owners and freelance operators can register as Sole Proprietors with their personal PAN and Aadhaar credentials.

## Category: Orders

### Q: How do I receive and accept machine rental bookings?
**A:** When a client books equipment matching your fleet, you receive instant SMS, push, and dashboard notifications. You can review the job location, duration, and rate before accepting or rejecting the order.

### Q: What should I do if a machine breaks down on site?
**A:** Immediately notify {{COMPANY_NAME}} support via the partner hotline within two (2) hours. Dispatch a mechanic for on-site troubleshooting. If repairs exceed 4 hours, a backup machine will be arranged to avoid project delays.

### Q: Who pays for equipment transport and mobilization?
**A:** Delivery mobilization and de-mobilization costs are paid by the client according to distance bands and are included in the order booking breakdown.

## Category: Payouts

### Q: How often are partner payouts credited?
**A:** Payouts are reconciled every Monday and Tuesday for the preceding week and credited directly to your bank account every Wednesday by 18:00 IST via IMPS or NEFT.

### Q: What is the commission rate deducted by {{COMPANY_NAME}}?
**A:** Standard partners pay a nominal 10.0% platform fee on gross rental billing. Higher volume partners qualifying for Preferred or Enterprise tiers enjoy reduced rates of 8.0% and 6.5% respectively.

### Q: How do I receive my TDS certificates (Form 16A)?
**A:** Form 16A certificates are generated quarterly and are directly downloadable in PDF format from the Payouts tab on your Partner Dashboard.

## Category: Referral

### Q: How does the Partner Referral Program work?
**A:** Every approved Partner receives a unique 6-8 character referral code. When a fellow machine owner registers using your code and completes their first 50 billing hours, both of you earn a bonus incentive credit.

### Q: Is there any limit to the number of partners I can refer?
**A:** No. You can refer unlimited partner fleet owners and earn incremental tier bonuses as your referred network expands.
