## 1. Booking Confirmation & Cancellation Windows

When a machine rental order is accepted by a Partner, logistical resources and client project plans are committed. This policy sets forth the cancellation protocol for both Partner-initiated and Client-initiated cancellations.

---

## 2. Partner-Initiated Cancellations

Partners are expected to maintain an acceptance fulfillment rate of at least 95%.
- **Cancellation > 24 Hours prior to Dispatch:** Permitted with zero financial penalty, subject to a maximum of two (2) occurrences per quarter.
- **Cancellation < 24 Hours prior to Dispatch:** A cancellation fee equal to 10% of the first day's rental value will be deducted from the partner's subsequent payout to offset client relocation costs.
- **No-Show on Delivery Date:** Failure to dispatch confirmed equipment without prior written notice will lead to automatic 14-day profile suspension and a 25% order penalty.

---

## 3. Client-Initiated Cancellations

If a client cancels a confirmed machine booking:
- **Prior to Equipment Dispatch:** The Partner receives no rental payment, but no penalties or costs are incurred.
- **En Route to Delivery Site:** The Partner receives 100% of the outbound mobilization/transportation fee plus 50% of the single-day rental fee.
- **Post Delivery / On-Site Cancellation:** The Partner is guaranteed a minimum billing of one (1) full shift or day of rental.

---

## 4. Force Majeure & Weather Exceptions

Cancellations due to severe floods, landslides, civil emergencies, or government transit curfews are categorized as Force Majeure and exempt from all penalties upon submission of regional regulatory notices.
