## 1. Overview & Commitment

**{{COMPANY_NAME}}** ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy rights of all Partners on our industrial machinery leasing network. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, protect, and share your personal, commercial, and operational information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and relevant data privacy regulations.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Identification Data
- Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Photograph.
- Mobile Phone Number, Emergency Contact Details, and Email Address.
- Proof of Identity: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License.

### B. Business & Operational Data
- Company Name, Business Registration Number (GST/MSME/CIN), and Registered Address.
- Bank Account Details: Account number, IFSC Code, Beneficiary Name, and Cancelled Cheque image.
- Police Verification Certificate (PVC) and criminal background attestation.
- Equipment Details: Registration plates, chassis numbers, telematics, and GPS operational telemetry.

### C. Technical & Usage Data
- IP Address, Device Fingerprint, Operating System, Browser User Agent.
- Access Timestamps, OTP verification timestamps, and Consent logs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is collected and processed exclusively for legitimate business purposes:
- **Authentication & Security:** Verifying partner identity via SMS OTP and validating KYC documents.
- **Order Fulfillment:** Dispatching rental inquiries, machine matching, and logistics coordination.
- **Financial Settlements:** Transferring weekly rental payouts and filing statutory tax returns (TDS).
- **Safety & Compliance:** Ensuring legal compliance with law enforcement and industrial safety standards.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:
- **Financial Institutions:** Payment gateway and banking partners for NEFT/RTGS automated payouts.
- **Verification Agencies:** Certified third-party vendors for Aadhaar KYC, PAN verification, and background checks.
- **Statutory Authorities:** Law enforcement or tax authorities when mandated by applicable judicial summons or law.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage

All partner data is encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Sensitive credentials such as OTPs and passwords are saved using cryptographic salted one-way hashes (SHA-256 and bcrypt). Access to production data stores is restricted through role-based access control (RBAC).

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Erasure

As a registered Partner, you have the right to:
- Access, review, and request correction of your profile and business details.
- Request an export of your transaction history and account telemetry.
- Request account deactivation and deletion in accordance with our Data Deletion Policy.
