## Category: Account

### Q: How do I create a customer account to hire equipment?
**A:** Simply register using your mobile phone number and verify it via the 6-digit SMS OTP. Complete your profile with your company name, GSTIN (optional), and job site delivery addresses.

### Q: Can an individual hire machinery without a registered company?
**A:** Yes. Private contractors, builders, and individuals can hire machinery by providing their Aadhaar or PAN details during registration.

## Category: Bookings

### Q: How far in advance should I book machinery?
**A:** We recommend booking at least 24 to 48 hours before planned site work to ensure optimal equipment matching and scheduled transport mobilization. Same-day emergency bookings are supported in metro hubs.

### Q: Does the rental include a certified operator?
**A:** You can choose between "Operator Included" (wet-lease) or "Bare Rental" (dry-lease) during checkout, based on your team's on-site requirements.

### Q: What happens if equipment malfunctions on the job site?
**A:** Report breakdowns immediately to our 24/7 hotline. A mobile technician will be dispatched to your site within 2 hours. If repairs exceed 4 hours, a replacement machine is arranged free of charge.

## Category: Payments

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** We accept all major corporate credit/debit cards, Net Banking, UPI, and corporate NEFT/RTGS bank transfers.

### Q: When do I receive the GST tax invoice?
**A:** GST invoices are generated upon booking completion and sent directly to your registered email and dashboard downloads.

## Category: Security Deposit

### Q: Why is a security deposit required?
**A:** Security deposits safeguard against structural machinery damage and unreturned accessories. The amount is determined based on equipment category and duration.

### Q: How quickly is the security deposit refunded?
**A:** Security deposits are initiated for refund within 48 hours following off-hire inspection and return confirmation.
