## 1. Scope of Rental Agreement

This Equipment Rental Agreement ("Agreement") governs the hire of heavy construction, earthmoving, material handling, and industrial machinery arranged through **{{COMPANY_NAME}}**.

---

## 2. Customer Site Obligations

The customer covenants and agrees that:
- The job site has sufficient bearing capacity, clearance, and access for heavy equipment mobilization.
- Safe on-site storage, parking, and security are provided during non-working hours.
- Machinery will only be used for lawful construction, excavation, and industrial operations.
- Fuel supplied (in dry-lease arrangements) conforms to standard commercial fuel grades (BS-VI / Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel).

---

## 3. Operator Provisions

- **Wet Lease (Operator Included):** The fleet partner provides certified operators. The customer manages daily on-site task allocation while respecting operator rest intervals.
- **Dry Lease (Customer Operator):** The customer must deploy only licensed and certified heavy machinery operators holding valid driving and safety credentials.
