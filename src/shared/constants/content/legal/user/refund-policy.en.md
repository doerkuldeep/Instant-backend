## 1. Security Deposit Refunds

Security deposits paid by customers for machinery rentals are held in dedicated escrow accounts.
- Upon equipment return and completion of off-hire inspection, the full security deposit is initiated for refund within forty-eight (48) hours.
- If verified equipment damages or missing accessories occur during customer possession, fair repair deductions are detailed and the remaining balance is promptly remitted.

---

## 2. Order Cancellation Refunds

If an order is cancelled prior to equipment mobilization:
- Full 100% refund of advance rental payments and security deposits.
- Refunds are processed via original payment methods within three (3) to five (5) business days.

---

## 3. Downtime & Incomplete Service Refunds

In the event of major mechanical failure occurring within four (4) hours of commencement that cannot be remedied or replaced, the customer is refunded the full daily rental charges for that shift.
