## 1. Rental Billing Structure

- **Standard Shift:** A standard rental shift comprises eight (8) working hours and one (1) hour break.
- **Overtime Hours:** Operational hours exceeding the standard shift are billed at proportional hourly overtime rates.
- **Logbook Verification:** Shifts and hour meter readings are tracked digitally via machine telematics and verified daily by customer site supervisors.

---

## 2. Security Deposit Terms

- Security deposits protect against structural equipment damage, excessive unauthorized wear, and unreturned implements.
- Deposits are computed based on machine category, replacement asset valuation, and rental duration.
- The balance is returned immediately following joint post-rental inspection.
