## 2. On-Site Safety Standards

- **Exclusion Zones:** Barricade swing radius zones and excavation pits to protect pedestrian personnel.
- **Underground Utilities:** Verify locations of underground electrical, gas, and telecommunications conduits prior to initiating excavation.
- **Overhead Power Lines:** Maintain minimum ten (10) feet clearance from all live high-voltage power lines.
- **PPE Requirements:** Hard hats, high-visibility apparel, steel-toe footwear, and hearing protection are mandatory in active machine operational zones.
