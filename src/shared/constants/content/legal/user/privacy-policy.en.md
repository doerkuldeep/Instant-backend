## 1. Introduction

**{{COMPANY_NAME}}** respects your privacy rights and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, storage, use, and protection of Customer data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** Name, mobile phone number, email address, and billing address.
- **Project Site Details:** Delivery site coordinates, project type, and on-site contact persons.
- **Payment & Invoicing Data:** GSTIN, transaction identifiers, and payment method tokens (handled securely via PCI-DSS certified payment processors).
- **Technical & Device Telemetry:** IP address, operating system, browser user-agent, and access logs.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed to:
- Authenticate customer logins via mobile OTP.
- Dispatch rental orders, coordinate logistics, and match machinery to job sites.
- Process rental invoices, tax compliance (GST), and security deposit refunds.
- Monitor safety, prevent fraud, and ensure dispute resolution.

---

## 4. Data Security

We enforce AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all sensitive customer data. Customer access tokens are signed with cryptographically secure HMAC secrets.
