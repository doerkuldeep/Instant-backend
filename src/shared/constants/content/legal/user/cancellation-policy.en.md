## 1. Booking Cancellation Guidelines

Customers may cancel or reschedule machinery bookings through the customer web portal or mobile app subject to the timelines set forth below.

---

## 2. Cancellation Timelines & Fee Schedule

- **More than 24 hours prior to scheduled delivery:** 100% refund of advance booking fees; zero cancellation charges.
- **Between 12 to 24 hours prior to delivery:** Cancellation fee of 10% of the first day's rental value.
- **Less than 12 hours or during transit:** Customer is responsible for actual round-trip mobilization transport costs incurred by the fleet owner.
- **After equipment arrives on-site:** Minimum charge of one (1) operational shift plus transit costs.

---

## 3. Rescheduling Bookings

Customers may reschedule a confirmed booking once without penalty provided notice is submitted at least eighteen (18) hours in advance of the original delivery window.
