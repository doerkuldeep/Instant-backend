## 1. Right to Data Erasure

Customers may request account closure and deletion of their personal information at any time upon completion of all active rentals and financial settlements.

---

## 2. Deletion Protocol

- Submit a deletion request from your verified mobile number or email to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
- Within thirty (30) days, all non-statutory personal identifiers, phone login tokens, and browsing telemetry are permanently purged.
- Statutory tax invoices and GST audit trails are retained for seven (7) financial years as required by Indian fiscal regulations.
